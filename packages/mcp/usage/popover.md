Use for small, non-blocking content anchored to a control: a filter panel, quick settings, extra detail.

- A list of actions: `Dropdown`. A text hint: `Tooltip`. A preview on hover: `HoverCard`. A task that needs the user's full attention: `Dialog`.
- It is not modal and has no focus trap; keep the content short, with at most a few controls.
- The `trigger` toggles it on click. Set `label` when the trigger text does not describe the panel.
