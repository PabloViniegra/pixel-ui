Use as the top navigation of a site or marketing-style app: brand, a few links, actions at the end.

- Dense application navigation with many destinations: use `Sidebar`.
- Mark the current page with `current` on `NavbarLink`, or on both `NavbarMenu` and its `NavbarMenuLink`.
- `NavbarMenu` groups related links behind a disclosure; keep top-level items to about five.
- It collapses behind a menu button below 640px on its own; do not build a separate mobile menu.
- Put buttons in `actions`, not among the links.
